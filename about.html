The man behind the music

The Man Behind the Music

Before the awards, performances, and recognition, there was Charles Hyman Jr.—a Suffolk, Virginia native with a passion for hip-hop. Known professionally as Chuck Black, his journey includes early musical success, incarceration, fatherhood, persistence, and a commitment to the culture that helped shape him.

His career was not built overnight or without interruptions. Through every chapter, music remained part of his life.

Early success

Early Success and Interrupted Momentum

In 2010, Chuck Black became a six-time champion of 103 JAMZ’s Battle of the Blocks with “Back On It,” produced by Lex Luger. The local buzz caught the attention of DJ Total Reck, who encouraged him to release a mixtape. That November, Chuck released Tha Product, hosted by DJ Total Reck and DJ Don Won.

Chuck was incarcerated shortly after the release and remained incarcerated until May 2012. Upon returning home, he joined the See Listers, Hoffla Boyz, and other Suffolk artists to help form Team U.S.A. (United Suffolk Alliance). The collective performed live and released The Team USA Mixtape. Two additional projects were recorded but never officially released.

Facing the past

Facing the Past and Moving Forward

In May 2013, Chuck was arrested and charged with two counts of attempted murder and other firearm-related offenses. His case went before a jury in a three-day trial held November 6–8, 2013. Working closely with his public defender, he supplied questions for his attorney to ask witnesses. The jury found him not guilty of all charges except the misdemeanor offense of shooting in a public place, for which it returned a guilty verdict. Following the trial, Chuck remained incarcerated on separate, unrelated charges until May 2015.

Further encounters with the legal system and probation restrictions limited his ability to pursue music as freely as he wanted, but he continued creating. In 2015 he collaborated with Kent Fi on 100 Jays, followed by Tha Product 1.5 in 2016, Tha Product 2 in 2017, and Green Dayz Purple Nightz in 2018.

Although earlier releases appeared under Chuck Black Tha Product, he had always performed locally as Chuck Black. In 2019, he simplified his streaming identity to Chuck Black and released Suffokation and BlackGold.

Fatherhood and responsibility

Fatherhood, Responsibility, and Persistence

The birth of his daughter in 2018 brought new priorities. Balancing fatherhood, employment, and everyday responsibilities meant less time for music, but stepping back never meant walking away. By 2020, when his daughter turned two, he began dedicating more time to his career again.

He continued performing across the 757, releasing freestyles and original music, and building his reputation through consistent independent work rather than a single breakout moment.

Recognition

Recognition Earned Through Consistency

Chuck Black was named VEER Hip Hop Artist of the Year in 2024 and again in 2026. To him, the honors represent recognition of his work and ability as an artist after years of dedication.

His live career has included opening opportunities involving Styles P, Beanie Sigel, Curren$y, Fabo, and other established acts. His catalog includes Berta’s Son, Tales From The Southside, The BlackPrint, the This Not Rap series, and the 2026 EP NOW LISTEN.

Across his releases, Chuck has expanded his songwriting and sound while retaining the identity and lyrical skill that established his name.

Giving the 757 a voice

More Than Music: A Voice for the 757

Noticing that hip-hop received limited coverage in local media, Chuck expanded his work beyond recording. Motivated by his love for 757 music and the artists behind it, he became a contributing hip-hop writer for VEER Magazine and founded 757Soundcheck.

Through music reviews and local coverage, he works to spotlight artists from Hampton Roads and document a scene he believes deserves greater visibility. His goal is not simply to promote himself, but to contribute to the community that helped shape him.

Beyond the artist

The Man Beyond the Artist

Behind the music, awards, and public appearances is Charles—a father and an individual shaped by responsibility, persistence, and personal growth. His past is part of his story, but it is not the entirety of who he is.

He wants to be recognized not only for his talent, but also for the person he is and the positive impact he can make on others. His music tells part of that story; his actions and contributions to his community tell the rest.
