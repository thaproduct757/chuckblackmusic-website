About Chuck Black

Artist. Writer. Host. Curator. Entrepreneur.

Independent by design

More than music

From Suffolk, Virginia, Chuck Black has built an independent career rooted in hip-hop, storytelling and community. His music draws on Southern and East Coast traditions while reflecting his own experiences and artistic evolution.

His releases include Berta’s Son, Tales From The Southside, The BlackPrint, the This Not Rap series and the 2026 EP NOW LISTEN.

Recognized as VEER Hip Hop Artist of the Year in both 2024 and 2026, Chuck also contributes hip-hop coverage to VEER Magazine and founded 757Soundcheck to spotlight music and voices from the region.

Whether recording, writing, hosting or curating, the mission is the same: create meaningful work, stay independent and represent the 757.
