New Releases from Sho Flacco, CmrnPrkr & Jo Bianca

By Chuck Black | 757Soundcheck

A look at Sho Flacco’s return, CmrnPrkr’s American Rapstar and Jo Bianca’s All Black.

Read the Full Article

The 757 continues to prove that talent isn't limited to one city, one sound, or one lane. This month, 757Soundcheck takes a look at three artists making noise across Hampton Roads. From Sho Flacco's return to music after years behind the camera, to CmrnPrkr's ambitious American Rapstar project, and Jo Bianca's confident new single, these releases offer listeners a snapshot of the creativity, versatility, and authenticity thriving throughout the region.

Hampton native Sho Flacco is a multi-talented artist, writer, director, and actor continuing to build a name for himself through both music and independent film. After stepping away from music releases for nearly seven years to focus on filmmaking and visual storytelling, Sho Flacco returned in January 2026 with his album So Help Me God, a project reflecting growth, experience, and personal evolution.

In May, he followed the album with the standout single "In My Shoes" featuring Loc Barz. Built around soulful production, the record finds Sho Flacco delivering honest verses rooted in real-life experiences, struggle, reflection, and perseverance. Loc Barz complements the track with a melodic hook that adds emotion and replay value. Together, the pair create a relatable listening experience that resonates beyond the music. Fans can stream So Help Me God, "In My Shoes," and explore his films Love Changes and Love Changes 2, currently available on Tubi and Apple TV.

Representing Suffolk, CmrnPrkr recently released American Rapstar under Revolt's 440 Artist and All Indie Recs. Released on May 11, the nine-track project opens with "Hello America," produced by Bailey Daniel. The record serves as an introduction to CmrnPrkr's vision while showcasing his confidence as he embraces the title of American Rapstar.

One of the project's most memorable moments comes when he references his co-sign from Teddy Riley, rapping, "Teddy Riley put me in the game so how can I fail." According to a press release from 440 Artist, the project serves as "a definitive sonic statement on the state of the union in 2026" while providing a rhythmic backdrop to the ongoing American journey. Tracks such as "Sweet Escape," "Day Like This," and "Run It Up" showcase his consistent flow and lyrical adaptability. Overall, American Rapstar is a smooth listen that demonstrates both ambition and artistic confidence.

Another emerging voice from Hampton, Jo Bianca, brings listeners her latest single, "All Black," a release that showcases an artist becoming increasingly comfortable in her own lane. Released in May, the record finds Jo Bianca leaning into a sleek, self-assured sound.

From the opening moments, she commands attention with a poised delivery and undeniable presence. Rather than relying on gimmicks, Jo Bianca allows her confidence, charisma, and lyricism to carry the record. The result is a song that feels authentic while still delivering the energy listeners expect from today's rap landscape.

"All Black" embraces themes of self-confidence, independence, and personal elevation. The accompanying visual perfectly complements the song's message, while the production provides a modern backdrop for her natural cadence and vocal performance. It's a balanced record that feels polished without sacrificing personality.

Whether you're looking for reflective storytelling, lyrical versatility, or confident new voices, these releases offer a glimpse into the talent continuing to emerge from Hampton Roads. As artists throughout the region keep pushing their craft forward, the 757 remains a place where creativity, authenticity, and independent ambition continue to thrive.
