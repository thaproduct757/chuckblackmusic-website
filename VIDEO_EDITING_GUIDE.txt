CHUCK BLACK WEBSITE — HOW TO UPDATE VIDEOS

Edit videos.js in your GitHub repository and commit the change.

OFFICIAL MUSIC VIDEOS: Add a {"title": "SONG NAME", "id": "YOUTUBE_ID"} entry to officialMusicVideos. The FIRST THREE entries also appear on the homepage.
INTERVIEWS: Add entries to interviewsAndMedia in the same format.
MYBLOCKLIVE: Update your existing YouTube playlist directly; the website player follows the playlist.

YouTube video ID: https://youtu.be/ABC123 -> ABC123.
Do not remove commas between entries; the last entry in an array must not have a trailing comma.

For future changes, update videos.js only. There is no need to upload the whole website ZIP again.
